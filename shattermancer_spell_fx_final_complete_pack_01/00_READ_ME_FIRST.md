# Shattermancer — Complete Spell FX Pack 01

This is the authoritative production delivery for the spell-casting animation project. It contains exactly 18 approved RGBA sprite sheets in `fx/`:

- six elemental Charge loops
- one shared element-neutral Travel loop
- six elemental Fracture impacts
- Resonate, Fizzle, Shatter, Shockwave, and Hitflash

The original commission listed 17 sheets. This final scope is 18 because `fx_resonate.png` was approved as a separate animation rather than scaling a Fracture impact. That distinction is intentional and required for outcome readability.

Read `docs/routing_reference.md` before placement. The Travel orientation rule and all outcome distinctions are stated there explicitly.
