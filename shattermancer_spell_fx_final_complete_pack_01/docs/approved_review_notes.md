## Charge review source

# Charge Review Notes

The six sheets share one timing grammar—form, tighten, peak, then breathe back—while retaining element-specific motion and silhouettes. They are not recolors of one generic orb.

| Element | Readable motion idea |
|---|---|
| Fire | upward-pulling flame and tightening embers |
| Water | rotating wet knot and orbiting droplets |
| Nature | leaf/root spiral with inward leaf fragments |
| Lightning | compressing crooked arcs and angular sparks |
| Ice | forming six-arm snowflake with frost motes |
| Shadow | collapsing void with inward smoke and dim motes |

Frame 12 and frame 1 are adjacent samples on a periodic curve. They deliberately are not duplicate held frames.


## Travel review source

# Travel Review Notes

The eight frames keep the leading knot stationary while the wake breathes in length, changes its wave phase, and sheds a small periodic set of trailing motes. Frame 8 and frame 1 are adjacent samples on the same periodic curve rather than duplicated held frames.

This is a shared, element-neutral asset. The game applies the dominant-incantation tint. No element-specific Travel variants and no discipline variants are required.


## Impact review source

# Impact Review Notes

All six sheets share the approved timing grammar but not a generic recolored silhouette. Substance remains readable throughout the peak and debris phases. Frames 7 and 8 hold the strongest beat; frame 14 is empty.

The full-strength Fracture remains visually distinct from the later shared Resonate sheet: Fracture bites at the center and bursts outward; Resonate will contain separated motes drifting apart with no central rupture.


## Feedback review source

# Review Notes

The four outcome reads are now mechanically distinct:

- **Fracture:** central bite and strong elemental burst.
- **Resonate:** five separated motes drift apart and fade.
- **All Resist / Fizzle:** cohesion fails, color drains, remnants sag downward.
- **Shatter:** a central plane visibly cracks and throws shards.

Shockwave and Hitflash remain universal overlays. Hitflash works over every current and future creature because it is a point flare rather than a silhouette.
