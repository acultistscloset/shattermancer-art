# Placement Notes

- `enemyHP.png` and `playerHP.png` are fill bodies only. The game scales their width from empty to full; do not add another frame or groove.
- The fill bodies are shown at full health in `battle_full.png` solely for fit review.
- `enemyBlock.png` and `playerBlock.png` share identical art intentionally. Runtime shield numbers are centered in their declared slots.
- `topBar.png` contains fixed Aurel and cog marks. The Aurel total is runtime text in the central recess; no number is baked in.
- `legendBtn.png` contains the fixed `?` mark and is placed at the exact template position on the Grimoire.
- All pieces use the existing template coordinates; no layout changed.
