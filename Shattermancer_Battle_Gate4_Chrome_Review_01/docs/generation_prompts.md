# Generation Record

Built-in image generation was used for the shared shield badge, combined top control, and legend control. The health-fill generation draft was rejected because it was too glossy and contained a false checkerboard; the final fills were adapted from the established restrained-crimson project source and conformed to the new canvases.

## Shared shield badge

Paint an isolated compact shield-shaped interior plate matching the approved status rails: thin quiet oxidized-brass edge, dark charcoal blackened-iron center, broad empty runtime-number area, true transparent exterior, no digit, text, glow, or heavy second frame.

## Aurel/settings top control

Paint one compact horizontal blackened-wood and leather control with a subdued Aurel sun medallion at left, empty runtime-number recess in the middle, and integrated settings cog at right; true transparent exterior; no digits, words, bright gold, glow, or excess ornament.

## Grimoire legend control

Paint one small dark charcoal/blackened-leather legend button with a thin oxidized-brass rim and a centered abraded antique-brass `?`; true transparent exterior; readable at 32 × 29 display pixels; no other text or ornament.

Generated false checkerboard on the top-control source was removed through the established deterministic alpha-extraction process before final sizing. The final production PNG contains genuine straight alpha.
