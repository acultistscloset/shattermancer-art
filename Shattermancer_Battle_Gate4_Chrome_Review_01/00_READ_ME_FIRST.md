# Shattermancer Battle — Gate 4 Chrome Review 01

This review package contains only the six remaining battle-screen utility assets:

- two stretchable health fills;
- two shield-number badges;
- the combined Aurel/settings top control; and
- the Grimoire legend control.

The empty health grooves remain part of the already-approved Gate 2 status rails. These health PNGs contain the fill body only and must be resized horizontally by the game as health changes.

All numbers remain runtime text. The only fixed baked marks are the Aurel emblem, settings cog, shield silhouette, and legend `?`; each is declared in the manifest.

Production stops here for the running-game fit test.
