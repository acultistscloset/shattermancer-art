# Shattermancer UI v2 --- Idea Parking Lot

Items here are **not approved requirements**.

## Visual identity candidate: physical wizard-interface metaphor

Major interfaces may feel like objects or magical systems the wizard
possesses, studies, manipulates or conjures rather than generic fantasy
HUD panels.

Possible vocabulary: grimoires, field journals, spell-preparation
surfaces, leather satchels, potion loops, brass instruments, wax seals,
parchment, runic mechanisms and ritual apparatus.

**Status:** EXPLORATORY\
**Review:** Phase 1

## Battle lower-area candidate

A future Battle v2 may treat prepared spells, potions and casting as a
wizard's working kit or ritual work surface rather than generic framed
slots.

**Status:** EXPLORATORY\
**Review:** Phase 3/4 after global identity is locked.

## Mandatory first-discipline Talent onboarding

When a player uses a discipline for the first time, the initial
Talent-selection state should be **mandatory onboarding**. The player must
select the required three talents and **explicitly confirm** them before
leaving the Talent screen. Back and other navigation out of this onboarding
state should be **disabled** until the requirement is completed.

The purpose is to teach the player that Talents must be selected and equipped.

**Note on current production:** production today **allows** the player to back
out of the initial Talent-selection state. This entry describes desired future
behaviour, **not** what the game currently does.

**Status:** FUTURE REQUIREMENT --- discovered during Phase 0 owner review\
**Not** a current-production fact. **Not** a LOCKED v2 design decision.\
**Review:** Phase 1
