# Phase 01 --- Visual Identity

**Status:** NOT STARTED

## Purpose

Define the governing visual identity and design laws before individual
screens are styled.

## Required work

Define approximately 8--12 durable laws covering what makes the
interface recognizably Shattermancer, pixel-art character/density,
thematic metaphors, world-art/UI relationship, ornament restraint,
hierarchy/readability, material vocabulary, and explicit anti-patterns.

## Prohibited work

Final per-screen geometry; production assets; implementation.

## Required deliverables

Approved identity statement; design laws; visual do/don't rules;
approved reference directions; resolved Phase-1 parking-lot ideas.

## Completion gate

Identity is specific enough to judge whether a future screen belongs to
Shattermancer without prescribing every screen's layout.
