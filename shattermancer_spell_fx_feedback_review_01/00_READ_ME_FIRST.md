# Shattermancer Spell FX — Shared Feedback Review 01

This review gate contains the final five shared production sprite sheets:

- `fx_resonate.png` — 12 frames, 4×3, 256×256 cells
- `fx_fizzle.png` — 12 frames, 4×3, 256×256 cells
- `fx_shatter.png` — 14 frames, 7×2, 320×320 cells
- `fx_shockwave.png` — 10 frames, 5×2, 384×384 cells
- `fx_hitflash.png` — 6 frames, 3×2, 320×320 cells

All play once at 24 fps and finish on a completely transparent final cell.

Resonate is a dedicated five-mote loose scatter and is not a scaled-down Fracture. Hitflash is a universal radial contact flare and contains no creature silhouette.

Production stops at this review gate before final 18-sheet consolidation.
