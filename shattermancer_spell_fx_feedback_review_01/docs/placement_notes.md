# Placement Notes

## Shared rules

- Read every sheet left-to-right, then top-to-bottom.
- Play once at 24 fps.
- Pivot is the exact center of every cell.
- The last frame of every sequence is completely transparent; do not add an implementation fade.
- Do not trim frames individually.

## Routing

- `fx_resonate.png`: right element, wrong position. Tint to the dominant/relevant element. Never substitute a scaled Fracture burst.
- `fx_fizzle.png`: all elements resisted. Do not tint into a satisfying elemental color; the sequence deliberately drains toward gray.
- `fx_shatter.png`: perfect incantation only.
- `fx_shockwave.png`: element-neutral overlay, tinted by the game where required.
- `fx_hitflash.png`: universal point-of-contact flare over any creature portrait; tint where required. It is not a creature mask.
