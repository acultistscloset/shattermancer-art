# Shattermancer Spell FX — Impact Review 01

This review gate contains the six finished elemental Impact/Fracture sprite sheets only.

- 14 frames per sheet
- 7 columns × 2 rows
- 320 × 320 pixels per cell
- 2240 × 640 pixels per sheet
- 24 fps, plays once
- drawn at 190 × 190 pixels
- frame 14 is completely transparent

These sequences begin at contact. The shared Travel sheet already covers the approach. They depict the full-strength Fracture result with a central bite and element-specific outward debris.

Production stops at this review gate. Resonate, Fizzle, Shatter, Shockwave, and Hitflash are not included or started here.
