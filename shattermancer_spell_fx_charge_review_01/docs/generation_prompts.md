# Generation Prompt Record

Built-in image generation was used to paint one transparent peak-form source per element. The sources were then animated deterministically into the exact production grids; generated source canvases are not production files.

Shared constraints: worn hand-painted dark-fantasy oil treatment; compact forming magic; transparent background; restrained brightness; effect only; no staff, wright, creature, hands, UI, frame, text, floor, or checkerboard.

- **fire:** dense upward flame nucleus, curling wisps, 5–7 embers; gathered heat rather than a launched fireball.
- **water:** visibly wet rotating liquid knot, curling ribbons and beads; no ice or crystal.
- **nature:** intertwined leaf-and-root spiral, vine tendrils, loose leaves and pollen.
- **lightning:** crooked arcs compressing inward, broken branches and sparse sparks; no bolt badge.
- **ice:** unmistakable six-arm snowflake forming from frost and snow motes; no gem/crystal silhouette.
- **shadow:** collapsing violet-black void with inward smoky ribbons and sparse motes; no galaxy or portal scene.
