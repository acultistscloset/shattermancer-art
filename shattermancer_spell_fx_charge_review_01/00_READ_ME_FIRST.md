# Shattermancer Spell FX — Charge Review 01

This review gate contains the six approved elemental Charge sheets only.

- 12 frames per sheet
- 4 columns × 3 rows
- 256 × 256 pixels per cell
- 1024 × 768 pixels per sheet
- 24 fps, looping
- effect anchored at the center of every cell
- effect only: no staff, wright, creature, UI, or text

The game should render the wright and staff separately, then place the charge effect at the staff-head attachment point. Do not trim individual frames or infer alternate pivots from their painted bounds.

Production stops at this review gate. Travel, Impact, Resonate, Fizzle, Shatter, Shockwave, and Hitflash sheets are not included or started here.
